Overview

Astrae built a design system inspired by their logo, creating a series of icons and illustrations to express their brand.

Our task was to bring them to life. We animated 70 unique assets, turning static visuals into a flexible motion library that could be used across their identity, website, and social channels.

The animations reinforce Astrae’s design language while adding energy, consistency, and playfulness. With this toolkit, Astrae now has a dynamic visual system that makes their brand more engaging and memorable
