Title

Hi on energy

Type

Brand video

Deliverables

Concept, Design, Brand video, SoMe

Overview

HI wanted to break through in a crowded market with a healthier alternative to traditional energy drinks. To tell that story, we created a motion brand video designed to energize their identity and communicate their unique positioning.

The video highlights HI’s focus on natural ingredients and health, while capturing the boldness and edge of the category. We built it as a flexible asset, tailored for use across multiple platforms: the website, pitch decks, events, and social media. By blending dynamic animation with clear messaging, HI now has a versatile tool that grabs attention, makes their values instantly clear, and strengthens their brand wherever it appears. A story in motion, designed to make HI unforgettable
